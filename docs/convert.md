# Convert files

The main window has three parts: **categories** on the left, your **files** in the middle, and **Output settings** on the right.

## Convert in four steps

1. Choose a category on the left: **Images**, **Audio**, **Video**, **Documents**, **PDF Tools** or **Compress**.
2. Drag files or whole folders into the window, or press **Browse files**. Files of another kind go to their own category automatically.
3. On the right, pick a **Format** (for example JPG, MP4 or PDF) and adjust the options.
4. Press **Convert**. The button shows how many files will be converted.

When it's finished, the **Conversion complete** screen shows every new file, how much space you saved, and buttons to open them or show them in their folder.

## Output settings

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Format | all | The file type to make |
| Quality / Max size | Images, Compress, Video | Quality of the result, or a [maximum file size](make-it-fit.md) |
| Bitrate | Audio | Sound quality for MP3, AAC and similar |
| Resolution | Video | 4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p or 360p, or the original |
| Max width | Compress | Make large photos smaller in pixels too |
| Edit | Images, Video, Audio | [Trim, watermark and rename](edit.md) |
| Save location | all | Next to the originals, or one folder you choose |

## Where converted files go

By default, new files are saved **next to the originals** with **SC** added to the name, for example `Holiday.heic` → `HolidaySC.jpg`. Nothing is ever overwritten. Switch off **Save at source folder** to save everything into one folder instead, and press **Change** to pick it.

## One-click presets

When files are added, a **One-click** row appears above the list: **Email**, **Instagram**, **Web**, **Compress**, **Thumbnail**, **Private copy**, **Under 100 KB**, **Form photo** for images, and similar presets for video, audio and documents. A preset converts straight away with settings made for that job. See [Presets and recipes](presets-recipes.md).

## Preview

Press **Preview** to look at a file before converting. For watermarks, the preview shows the watermark exactly where it will be.

## Pause and cancel

While converting you can **Pause** (the current file finishes first) or **Cancel**. When you cancel, the half-made file is removed, and files that already finished are kept.

## Tips

- Drop a folder to add every supported file inside it.
- iPhone **HEIC** and camera **RAW** photos convert to JPG, PNG, WebP and others.
- For video, S Converter uses your **NVIDIA, Intel or AMD graphics card** automatically, which is usually several times faster.
