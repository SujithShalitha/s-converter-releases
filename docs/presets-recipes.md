# Presets and recipes

## Built-in presets

A preset is a ready-made job. Add files and press one in the **One-click** row; it starts straight away.

| Images | Video | Audio | Documents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email: JPG, max 1600 px | Shrink: 720p MP4 | MP3 320k | To PDF |
| Instagram: fits 1080 × 1350 | WhatsApp: 480p MP4 | Voice Note: small mono MP3 | Just Text |

The same presets are on the **Presets** page of the [Quick Convert wheel](quick-convert.md).

## Save your own preset

1. Open **Images**, **Audio**, **Video**, **Documents** or **Compress**, and add a file.
2. Set things up on the right: format, quality or [max size](make-it-fit.md), resolution, watermark.
3. At the end of the One-click row, press **＋ Save**.
4. Give it a name, for example *Blog photos*, and press **Save**.

Your preset appears in the One-click row and on the wheel. To delete it, point at it and press the small **×**. You can keep up to 30.

## Recipes: several steps in one click

The **Save as preset** window can also add steps, which makes your preset a recipe:

- **Also rename the files**: when a [Rename](edit.md) pattern is set (for example `Trip-{n}`), the recipe uses it every time.
- **When it's done**: keep the files, **put them all in one ZIP**, or **put them all in one PDF** (for photos).

For example, *Trip album* = resize to 1600 px → rename Trip-001, 002… → one PDF. One click does it all, and only the final ZIP or PDF is kept; the in-between files are removed.

Recipes work the same on the wheel and from the app.
