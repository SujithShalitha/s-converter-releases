# Make it fit

Online forms, email and chat apps all have limits: *under 100 KB*, *600 × 600 pixels*, *no more than 25 MB*. S Converter can hit those limits for you.

## A maximum file size

1. Open **Images**, **Compress** or **Video** and add your files.
2. On the right, switch **Quality** to **Max size**.
3. Pick a limit: 50 KB to 5 MB for photos, 8 MB to 100 MB for video. Or choose **Custom…** and type your own.
4. Press **Convert**.

**Photos** get the best quality that fits under the limit. S Converter only makes the picture smaller in pixels if lowering the quality alone isn't enough. **Videos** get a bitrate worked out from their length; if the first try lands slightly over, S Converter tries again a little smaller.

If a limit is impossible (for example 1 KB for a large photo), S Converter saves the smallest version it can make and tells you.

The summary above the **Convert** button shows the limit, for example *3 files · 5 MB → JPG, under 100 KB*.

## Presets that fit

- **Under 100 KB** (images): a JPG under 100 KB, for upload forms.
- **Email** (video): an MP4 under 20 MB, which fits most email services.

These are also on the [Quick Convert wheel](quick-convert.md).

## Photos for forms

Visa, passport and exam applications usually want a photo at an exact size. Press **Form photo** in the One-click row, choose a size, and press **Make the photos**:

| Choice | Size | File size |
|---|---|---|
| US visa | 600 × 600 px (square) | under 240 KB |
| Passport 35 × 45 mm | 413 × 531 px (at 300 dpi) | under 500 KB |
| 2 × 2 inch | 600 × 600 px | under 500 KB |
| Custom size | your width × height | your limit |

The photo is cropped to the exact size around the face (S Converter looks for the most interesting part of the picture), and saved as a JPG small enough to upload.

> Rules change and differ between countries and forms. Always check the form's own instructions, and use **Custom size** to match them exactly.

The US visa and DV lottery numbers come from the U.S. Department of State: a square JPEG, 600 × 600 pixels, no more than 240 KB.

## Tips

- Start from the best original you have. A sharp photo stays sharp at a small size.
- For forms that want a plain background, take the photo against a plain wall; S Converter doesn't change the background.
- Save your own limits as a [preset](presets-recipes.md), for example *Under 200 KB* for a portal you use often.
