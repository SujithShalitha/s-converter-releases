# Quick Convert wheel

The fastest way to convert: select files where they already are, press a shortcut, click a format. A wheel opens right at your mouse, and S Converter's window never has to open.

## Use the wheel

1. Select one or more files or folders in **File Explorer**, on the **desktop**, or in an **Open or Save dialog** of any program.
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd>. The wheel opens where your mouse is.
3. Click a format, such as **JPG**, **MP4** or **PDF**. Or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> for the **Presets** page.
4. Watch the progress in the middle. The new files appear next to the originals.

S Converter waits in the system tray, so the shortcut works any time after you've opened it once.

## On the wheel

| Key or action | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> or mouse wheel | Switch between Formats and Presets |
| <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> | Pick a slice |
| Arrow keys, then <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Choose a slice with the keyboard |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Go back, or close the wheel |
| Drag the centre | Move the wheel |

The wheel only offers what fits the files you selected: formats for photos, video, audio, documents and PDFs; extract and convert for archives; ZIP, 7Z and RAR for folders.

## Paste as… (the clipboard)

Took a screenshot, or copied an image from a web page? Press the Quick Convert shortcut **with nothing selected**: the wheel opens for the image on the clipboard. Pick a format, and the result is **copied back to the clipboard**, ready to paste into a chat, an email or a document. A copy is saved in **Pictures\S Converter\Clipboard**.

You can also right-click S Converter's tray icon and choose **Convert the clipboard image**.

## Several images into one PDF

Choose **PDF** with several images selected, and the wheel asks: **One PDF** or **Separate**? For one PDF, the **Page order** screen lists the pages with thumbnails, in name order (1, 2, 3 … 10, not 1, 10, 2). To rearrange:

- drag a page up or down,
- use the ▲ ▼ buttons, or <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>↑</kbd>/<kbd>↓</kbd>,
- press **Reverse** to flip the order,

then press **Create PDF**.

## Form photos on the wheel

Choose **Form photo** on the Presets page and the wheel asks which size: **US visa**, **Passport 35 × 45 mm** or **2 × 2 inch**. See [Make it fit](make-it-fit.md).

## It learns your favourites

Formats and presets you pick at least three times move to the front of the wheel, up to three of them, most-used first. Everything else keeps its usual place, so the wheel stays familiar.

## More shortcuts

In **Settings → More shortcuts** you can add two more keys, for example <kbd>F7</kbd> and <kbd>F9</kbd>:

- **Presets** opens the wheel straight on its Presets page.
- **Repeat last** does the last thing you chose for that kind of file again, without opening the wheel.

You can also change the main shortcut, for example to <kbd>F8</kbd>, or switch it off. See [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md).

## Limits

- It reads selections from File Explorer, the desktop and the standard Windows Open and Save dialogs. Programs with their own custom file browser can't be read; use the [right-click menu](right-click.md) there.
- If file extensions are hidden and a folder has two files with the same name (photo.jpg and photo.png), the wheel takes both.
